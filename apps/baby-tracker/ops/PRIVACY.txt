LUMI Baby Tracker — personal privacy and permissions (offline)

Scope: one caregiver, one child, one device. No account and no family cloud.

Data on device
- Care diary, preferences, recovery checkpoints, and encrypted portable backups
  stay on this device (or in files the caregiver explicitly exports).
- Native builds store the diary in an encrypted SQLite database. The web build
  keeps data in origin-scoped browser storage; same-origin scripts can still
  reach it — treat the browser profile as the trust boundary.
- App lock (when enabled) uses the platform secure store / biometric prompt.
  A lock failure is reported; the app does not silently drop to an unlocked
  memory substitute after the caregiver turned locking on.

Permissions (requested only when the related feature is used)
- Notifications: local care reminders scheduled on the device OS. Unsupported
  environments do not pretend delivery was granted.
- Face ID / biometrics (iOS): unlock the app when the caregiver enabled the lock.
- Files / sharing: choose a destination for an encrypted backup export, or pick
  a previously exported archive to restore. The app does not upload backups.

What this personal build does not do
- No sign-in, analytics backend, remote sync, or store of another phone’s diary.
- “Uloženo” means the local write finished — not an independent off-device copy.
- Dictation, if used, is the system keyboard feature, not an in-app cloud service.

Support reports
- Settings → Diagnostika can export a redacted support report. Notes, dates of
  birth, and diary backups are not attached automatically.

Deletion
- Removing the app removes its on-device sandbox. Exported backup files the
  caregiver saved elsewhere are not deleted by uninstalling the app.
