# Family incident runbooks

Invariant: isolate the remote service without wiping phones or locking local history.
No remote kill switch may delete the device diary.

## token_leak

1. Rotate the leaked secret offline; revoke sessions at the IdP.
2. Capture request IDs only — never paste access/refresh tokens into chat.
3. Confirm caregivers can still open the local tracker without the API.

## foreign_access

1. Revoke invitation hashes / freeze membership; keep opaque audit IDs.
2. Force admin re-auth from a trusted device.
3. Do not export notes or DOB into the incident ticket.

## database_failure

1. Fail `/ready`; stop writers that would enlarge corruption.
2. Restore logical dumps after applying the current erasure registry.
3. Clients keep offline capture until readiness returns.

## malicious_update

1. Pin the last known-good image digest; block the bad tag in the registry.
2. Refuse binary rollback that opens a newer incompatible schema.
3. Embedded content packs stay on-device; refuse unsigned activations.

## bad_content_pack

1. Keep the previously verified local catalog.
2. Publish only a higher signed generation after review.
3. Care logging remains available offline.

## vps_total_outage / disk_full / identity_loss

Follow family-ops-runbook.txt and restore-runbook.txt. Measure actual RPO/RTO for the family release gate. Off-host alert delivery is mandatory for total host loss.
